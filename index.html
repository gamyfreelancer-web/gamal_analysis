About Me نبذة عني

Your Photo صورتك

A passionate Data Analyst with over 2 years of experience in transforming complex datasets into actionable insights. Graduated from the Faculty of Navigation Technology and Space Sciences at Beni Suef University with an Excellent grade in 2026.

My graduation project involved analyzing NASA POWER satellite data to predict solar radiation for optimal solar panel placement — combining space technology with data science.

Proficient in Python, SQL, Power BI, Excel, and various analytics tools. I love turning messy data into clear stories that drive decisions.
