Solar Radiation Prediction & Forecasting التنبؤ بالإشعاع الشمسي (GHI & DHI Forecasting)

This project represents the backbone of my work and is vital for countries investing in the fuel of the future: "Solar Radiation". It served as my university graduation project, developed within a 5-member team, and was awarded an Excellent grade. Key Objectives & Highlights: Empowering Solar Engineers & Project Owners: Identifies optimal geographical locations based on solar irradiance intensity, temperature, humidity, wind patterns, and other atmospheric factors affecting PV panel performance. 5-Year Forecasting & Feasibility Studies: Predicts solar radiation for specific locations up to 5 years into the future, helping investors and stakeholders conduct accurate feasibility studies and ROI analyses. Advanced AI with NASA POWER Data: High-performance AI models trained on extensive meteorological and solar historical datasets from NASA POWER spanning from 1990 to 2025, ensuring high predictive accuracy. Forecasting 3 Critical Factors: Accurately derives Solar Irradiance, Wind, and Temperature. This model specifically benefits Gulf countries given their climate profile and ambitious renewable energy strategies. There are many more analytical and technical details behind this work.
